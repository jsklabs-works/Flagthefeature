The Australian payments landscape

Clearing happens on many rails run by different bodies. Settlement between banks almost always ends in RITS, across Exchange Settlement Accounts at the Reserve Bank.

ASICConduct and licensing; administers the ePayments Code.
ACCCCompetition; enforces against excessive surcharges.

Core concepts every payment shares

Every payment is two flows travelling together: information (instructions, approvals, files) and value (money moving between accounts, finally between banks at the RBA). Select a stage to see what happens in it.

Terms that decide how a rail behaves

Push vs pull

Push: the payer tells their bank to send money (Osko, direct credit, BPAY). Pull: the payee draws money under an authority (direct debit, cards). PayTo is payee-initiated but executes as a push from the payer's bank.

Clearing vs settlement

Clearing is exchanging payment details and working out who owes whom. Settlement discharges those obligations by moving money between banks' ESAs.

Gross vs net

Gross: each payment settles on its own (NPP, HVCS). Net: obligations are offset and only the difference moves (cards, BECS, BPAY, cheques).

Real-time vs deferred

Whether interbank settlement happens at the moment of the payment (NPP, 24/7) or later in batches (BECS intraday sessions, cards next business day).

Finality

Settlement in RITS is final and irrevocable, protected by the Payment Systems and Netting Act 1998. Earlier steps (card approval, provisional credits) are not.

Central bank vs commercial bank money

Your account balance is a claim on your bank. Banks settle with each other in ESA balances at the RBA.

Settlement infrastructure: RITS and ESAs

RITS is the Reserve Bank's real-time gross settlement system. Whatever rail clears a payment, the final interbank transfer happens here.

ESA

An Exchange Settlement Account at the RBA. Held by ADIs and some approved entities. Others settle through a sponsor or agent bank such as Cuscal or Indue.

RTGS engine

Settles each payment individually if the paying bank has enough ESA funds; otherwise the payment queues. Used by HVCS, Austraclear and settlement batches.

LVSS

Low Value Settlement Service. Takes File Settlement Instructions for BECS, cheques, card and ATM obligations and BPAY, and settles them in scheduled net batches.

FSS

Fast Settlement Service. Settles every NPP payment individually, in real time, 24/7/365, against a dedicated ESA allocation.

Intraday liquidity

Banks borrow ESA funds intraday from the RBA through repurchase agreements (repos), and keep liquidity in FSS for nights and weekends.

Batch feeders

CHESS (equities), PEXA (property) and card scheme batches settle in RITS so that cash moves at the same time as securities or title.
